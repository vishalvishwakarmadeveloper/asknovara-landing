02What we collect

Information you give us

When you use the "Just ask" form on our website, we collect:

  • Name and email address (required), so we can reply to you.
  • Company name (optional).
  • What you want to do - build a new SaaS product, grow an existing SaaS, set up cloud infrastructure or add AI automations.
  • Budget range you select.
  • Your idea - the description you type. Please do not include sensitive personal data (for example health information) or anything you need protected by an NDA until one is in place.

If you go on to book a 1:1 session, email us or work with us, we will also hold the correspondence, meeting notes and any project information you share.

Information collected automatically

Our website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not set cookies (see our Cookie Policy). As with any website, the servers that deliver the page record standard technical data - your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request - for security and to keep the site running. Some page assets are loaded from third-party providers listed in section 6, and those providers receive the same technical data when your browser requests them.

04Your ideas and confidentiality

We know an early-stage idea is valuable. Everything you describe in the form or in a 1:1 session is used only to assess and discuss your project, is shared only with the Ask Novara team members working on it, and is never used to build a competing product. You keep all rights in your idea. If you want a formal non-disclosure agreement, ask and we will sign one before you share details.

05Who we share it with

We share personal data only where needed for the purposes above:

  • Service providers who host our website, email and business systems on our behalf. They act under our instructions and under contracts that require them to protect your data.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, where necessary.
  • Authorities where we are required to by law.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is reorganised or sold, under the same protections set out here.

07International transfers

Some of our service providers, including several of the website providers in section 6, operate servers outside the UK, for example in the United States, the European Economic Area or India. Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on a UK adequacy regulation, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or another safeguard permitted by UK GDPR.

08How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become a project: deleted 24 months after our last contact with you.
  • Client and contract records: kept for 6 years after the end of the engagement, to meet UK tax, accounting and limitation-period requirements.
  • Technical request logs: kept by our hosting and delivery providers for their standard short log period, normally no longer than 30 days.

You can ask us to delete your enquiry sooner at any time.

09How we protect it

Our website is served over HTTPS. Access to enquiries and client data is limited to the team members who need it, protected by individual accounts and multi-factor authentication where available. No system is completely secure, but if a breach affecting your data occurs we will notify you and the ICO where the law requires it.

10Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your data where we no longer have a reason to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to our use of it, including any use based on legitimate interests.
  • Portability - receive data you gave us in a reusable format.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting earlier use.

To use any of these rights, email ***********. It is free, and we will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

11Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

12Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

13Changes to this policy
