01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage and tracking pixels. Under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), a website must ask for your consent before using any of these, unless they are strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for.
